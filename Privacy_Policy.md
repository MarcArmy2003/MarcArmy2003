# Privacy Policy for TERA Alignment GPT
**Last Updated: July 19, 2025**

This Privacy Policy describes how TERA Alignment GPT ("the GPT," "we," "us," or "our") processes information when you interact with it.

---

## 1. Information We Process

When you interact with the GPT, we process information in the following ways:

- **User Input/Queries**  
  We receive and process the text and other data you input into the GPT (your questions, commands, and any files you upload or reference). This information is necessary for the GPT to understand your requests and generate responses.

- **Responses Generated by the GPT**  
  The text and content generated by the GPT in response to your queries, including crosswalks, executive summaries, policy memos, implementation trackers, and evidentiary tables.

- **Interaction Data**  
  This GPT does not connect to any external third-party services or APIs for data retrieval or action beyond its internal knowledge base. All processing occurs within the GPT’s environment on the OpenAI platform, utilizing the uploaded documents, metadata (titles, dates, context), analytic inputs, and directory structure defined in `folder_map.txt` for its "persistent memory" and "fast recall."

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The information we process is used solely for the following purposes:

- To understand your requests and provide accurate, relevant, and evidence-backed outputs, functioning as an analytic aide and policy writer for TDWG–TERA data, automation, and modernization integration.
- To support decision-making for the TERA Data Working Group (TDWG), Veterans Benefits Administration (VBA), Veterans Health Administration (VHA), Office of General Counsel (OGC), and Office of the Secretary for Veterans Affairs (OSVA).
- To interpret multi-format source files (Word, PDF, PowerPoint, Excel) and produce deliverables in markdown or Word-ready formats aligned with VA Executive Style standards.
- To improve the performance and accuracy of the GPT through analysis of interactions (e.g., identifying common queries or areas for better response generation). This typically involves aggregated and anonymized data where possible.

---

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

- **Platform Provider**  
  Your interactions with the GPT, including your inputs and the GPT’s responses, are processed by the underlying AI platform provider (e.g., OpenAI, Google). Their privacy policies govern how they handle this data.

- **Third-Party Actions/APIs**  
  As stated, this GPT does **not** connect to external third-party services or APIs. Therefore, no information is shared with external third-party services via "Actions."

---

## 4. Data Retention

The underlying AI platform provider retains interaction data according to their own data retention policies. We, as the creator of this specific GPT, do not store or retain your individual interaction data outside of the platform environment.

---

## 5. Your Rights

Your rights regarding your data are primarily governed by the privacy policy of the underlying AI platform provider (e.g., OpenAI, Google). Please refer to their respective privacy policies for information on how to access, correct, or delete your data.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

This GPT is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.
