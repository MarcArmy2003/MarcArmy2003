# Gillon Marchetti

U.S. Army veteran, attorney, and federal policy analyst. Founder of
[Veteran Analytics LLC](https://github.com/VeteranAnalyticsLLC).

I spent years inside the VA benefits system and years watching people try to
get through it from the outside. The law is public. The regulations are public.
The policy manuals are public. Almost none of it is findable when you actually
need it. That gap is what I build against.

## Rangelight

**[rangelight.org](https://rangelight.org)** — Independent intelligence for the
veteran community. Ask a question about benefits, health, education, or housing
and get an answer grounded in the statute, regulation, or policy it came from,
with the source attached.

For current availability and supported capabilities, visit the product site.
Repository documentation is not a live service-status report.
